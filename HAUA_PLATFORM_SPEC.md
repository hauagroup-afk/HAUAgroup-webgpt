# HAUA GROUP — Platform rebuild specification

This project is a parent corporate site plus four distinct digital platform experiences. Treat each branch as its own product, not as a simple company information tab.

## Corporate parent
- Preserve the official HAUA GROUP logo supplied by the owner.
- Premium, responsive, accessible landing page with four clearly differentiated destinations.
- Corporate story, brand directory, contact, multilingual EN/BN/AR including RTL.

## Ruhama Technology — App Store (exclusive developer: HAUA GROUP)
- App discovery, categories, search/filter, app detail pages and version information.
- Download links (external or supplied), preview/screenshots/video.
- UI model/template gallery, previews and publishing workflow.
- Sponsored listings/video advertisement placements.
- Ratings/reviews, favorites, reporting and moderation.
- Admin publication/update/advertisement controls. Do not allow third-party developer accounts.

## LAZEEZ FOOD — marketplace
- Product categories, listing/detail views, image/video, price, stock and search/filter.
- Cart, checkout, delivery/order tracking, customer reviews.
- Sponsored and promoted listings.

## Rifa Aiman's Attire — marketplace
- Fashion catalog, collection/category, sizes, colors, materials, stock and prices.
- Product media/details, cart, checkout, order tracking, reviews.
- Sponsored/promoted placements.
- Use owner's second Rifa logo on white background without distorting the logo.

## WAHA AGRI — marketplace
- Produce, food and agriculture supplies catalog, search/filter, images/video, pricing.
- Nutrition/benefits, safety concerns, disadvantages/limitations and storage/use information when applicable; do not present unsupported health claims as facts.
- Cart/checkout/order tracking/reviews and sponsored listings.

## Shared functionality
- Mobile-first responsive layout, EN/BN/AR and Arabic RTL.
- User accounts, wishlist/cart, orders, delivery, contact/inquiries.
- Role-scoped admin panel, content moderation, catalog/advertising analytics.
- Security: server-side price/role validation, auth, rate limiting, RLS and auditability.
- Start with free-tier hosting/services and COD/manual payment; no paid gateway or domain without authorization.

## Build and release plan
1. Homepage positioning and official brand asset integration.
2. Independent brand portal navigation and catalog/search/details.
3. Customer accounts/cart/checkout/order management.
4. Admin publishing, promotions, moderation and analytics.
5. Security review, functional/mobile testing and deployment verification.

## Verification honesty
A GitHub commit does not confirm that Cloudflare Pages deployed or that live behavior passed. Report deployment and testing only when verified.
